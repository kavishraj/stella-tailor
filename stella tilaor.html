Meet Stella

GOT QUESTIONS?

Have Questions? We're Happy to Help.

What clothes does Stella stitch? +
Stella specializes in women's tailoring including saree blouses, frocks, churidhars, and custom designs. From traditional to modern styles, she can create any outfit according to your measurements and preferences.
Does Stella stitch men's clothing? +
No, Stella's tailoring service is exclusively for women's clothing. She specializes exclusively in women's outfits to ensure the highest quality and attention to detail.
How many years of experience does Stella have? +
Stella has over 12 years of professional tailoring experience, having worked with thousands of customers and created countless beautiful outfits.
Is Stella professionally trained? +
Yes, Stella has completed a Professional Diploma in Tailoring and continuously builds her expertise by working with different fabrics, designs, and styles.
Is the ₹1 Priority Preference compulsory? +
No, the ₹1 Priority Preference is completely optional. You can register for free normal registration, and Stella will review your request personally. The priority preference is just an optional add-on if you'd like expedited attention.
How do I place a stitching request? +
Simply fill out the registration form below with your name, email, and phone number. Tell us what you'd like to stitch, and Stella will personally get in touch with you to discuss your requirements and provide a quote.
Can I request a particular design? +
Absolutely! Stella creates custom designs based on your preferences. You can share reference images, describe your ideas, or even design your outfit together. Every piece is created specifically for you.
